About us

Small studio, big care.

Lumière started with one chair and a simple idea: a beauty appointment should feel like a break, not a rush. Today our small team of qualified therapists looks after brows, lashes, nails and skin for the people of Surry Hills and beyond.

We keep bookings unhurried, use gentle, good-quality products, and always tell you the price before we start.
